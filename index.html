FIELD MANUAL

Contain. Recover. Restore.

  1. Isolate compromised machines to stop lateral spread.
  2. Seal the backup vault before it becomes infected.
  3. Restore an isolated machine using a verified sealed backup, or rebuild it without a backup.
  4. Reconnect clean machines to restore availability. Harden healthy machines against future incidents.
  5. Use your limited response points before advancing a cycle. Start the clock for real-time play, or advance manually.
  6. Win by sealing the vault, restoring all services and verified backups, surviving all intrusion waves and maintaining two clean cycles. If backups were damaged, select the vault and verify a fresh backup once all services are online.

Each cycle represents five seconds of the simulated deadline. Infected machines can encrypt after two cycles. A sealed vault stays offline intentionally.
